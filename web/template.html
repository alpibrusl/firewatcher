Wildfire Risk & Prevention Analytics Platform

A design for an open-data platform that harmonises Spain's fragmented wildfire record — EGIF, EFFIS, Copernicus, PNOA-LiDAR — into reproducible, analysis-ready risk products: from a deduplicated 1968–present fire record to ignition hazard models, burn probability, and fuel-treatment optimisation.
