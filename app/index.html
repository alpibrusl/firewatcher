The platform behind this page

Firewatcher is a design for an open-data wildfire analytics platform for Spain. Its six products:

  • P1 Harmonised fire record, 1968–present (EGIF ⇄ EFFIS)
  • P2 Daily fire-danger reanalysis, 1 km, 1980–present
  • P3 25 m fuel & structure layer from PNOA-LiDAR + MFE
  • P4 Calibrated daily ignition-hazard model
  • P5 Monte Carlo burn probability & exposure
  • P6 Fuel-treatment placement optimiser
